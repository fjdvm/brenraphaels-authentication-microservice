## Remaining authentication benchmark

The supplied Administrator credentials were verified successfully on the live Portal. The staged host's generic **Invalid credentials** message did not identify the real cause: a direct provider probe with that Origin returned HTTP 403, `INVALID_ORIGIN`. Neon Auth's trusted-domain list contains only `https://br-auth-three.vercel.app`. Temporary registration of the exact staged origin, followed by its removal after measurement, is awaiting operator approval. No trusted-domain configuration has been changed.

Successful staged sign-in, sign-out, change-password, forgot-password, and reset-password timings therefore remain outstanding. Credentials and email access are no longer the blocker. #89's full after-region timing criterion is still open.

The test Customer Profiles AccessGrant was revoked and `2026-PR-002` was soft-deleted through the Portal after measurement. Audit and grant history remain intact. The same test User can be restored for the remaining password measurements once the staged origin is approved; its private inbox remains available for that continuation.

During failed sign-in attempts, runtime logs recorded `Failed to record audit event` with `TimeoutError` in `sin1`. This is an observed dependency failure, not evidence of a successful sign-in duration or proof that the region caused it. Investigate it when repeating the authenticated benchmark; it could affect action timings and audit availability.
